Third-party models used by Pal

Pal downloads these models from their sources on first start. They are not part of the app package. Each is provided
by its authors "as is", without warranties, as set out in its license. Their authors do not endorse Pal.


Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3 (speech recognition)
  Author:  NVIDIA
  Source:  https://huggingface.co/nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3
  License: CC-BY-4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: int8 ONNX conversion by istupakov, used without further changes,
           https://huggingface.co/istupakov/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-onnx

Pocket TTS (speech synthesis)
  Author:  Kyutai
  Source:  https://huggingface.co/kyutai/pocket-tts-without-voice-cloning
  License: CC-BY-4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: Pal quantizes the weights to 8 bits when loading them.

Qwen3.5 2B and Qwen3.5 0.8B (language model)
  Author:  Qwen team, Alibaba Cloud
  Source:  https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B
           https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B
  License: Apache-2.0, https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B/blob/main/LICENSE
  Changes: GGUF quantization by Unsloth, used without further changes,
           https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.5-2B-GGUF
           https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.5-0.8B-GGUF

Smart Turn v3 (end of turn detection)
  Author:  Pipecat
  Source:  https://huggingface.co/pipecat-ai/smart-turn-v3
  License: BSD-2-Clause, https://opensource.org/license/bsd-2-clause
  Changes: none
